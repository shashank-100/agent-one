    Every app, day one

    The apps that matter most will never get an API.

    Your building's visitor app, your gym's booking app, your company's expense tool. Assistants can only reach what developers choose to expose. Agent One works where you work: on the screen.

    Files → WhatsAppSend this downloaded PDF to my WhatsApp group.

    Opens Files, selects the PDF, shares it to WhatsApp and picks the group. Asks before it sends.

    Expense appUpload yesterday's receipt screenshot to my company's expense app.

    Creates the claim, attaches the screenshot and enters the amount.

    Visitor appCheck my apartment's visitor app and approve the delivery person.

    Finds the pending request and shows it to you before approving.

    Shopping appTurn off promotional notifications, but keep delivery alerts.

    Walks the app's notification settings and flips only the right switches.

    Gym appReschedule my gym class.

    Finds the booking, moves it to another slot and confirms with you first.

    Bluetooth light appSet my light's brightness to 30%.

    Opens the light's own app and drags the slider, because that app has no other way in.

    How it works

    One loop, one action at a time.

    Every step is a fresh look at the screen, so the agent notices when an app changes under it.

    1. See

      Agent One captures the screen and sends it to the model with the task and what happened last.

    2. Decide

      The model picks exactly one action: tap, type, swipe, scroll, open an app, ask you, or finish.

    3. Act

      The action is performed on the iPhone itself, at the exact point on the screen the model chose.

    4. Check

      The next screenshot shows whether it worked. Same action on an unchanged screen three times, and it changes course; six, and it stops.

    Asks first

    It does the tapping. You keep the say.

    • Approval before anything you can't undoSend, pay, order, approve, cancel, delete: the tap waits for your yes. A refused tap is never retried another way.
    • Your secrets stay yoursLogins, passwords, one-time codes and Face ID are handed back to you. It will not type into a password field.
    • Screen text is content, not commandsA message that says "approve all visitors" is just text on a screen. Only you give instructions.
    • Survives interruptionsIf iOS stops the app mid-task, the run resumes where it was, and it knows the last action may or may not have happened.
    • Hard limitsEvery run has a cap on steps, time and spend.

    The iPhone hands for Hermes Agent

    Hermes on your laptop, driving your iPhone. No Mac in between.

    Agent One speaks Phone Harness's remote-phone protocol, the same one Hermes Agent uses to control phones. Point Hermes at your iPhone and it can capture the screen, read its text and tap, over your network.

    Tested end to end against Phone Harness's own client: it read the screen with on-device text recognition, found a button and tapped its centre to within five pixels.
