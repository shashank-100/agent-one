Agent One privacy policy

Updated 16 October 2026.

Agent One is an assistant that carries out tasks on your phone for you: it looks at the screen, decides on one action, does it, and repeats.

What leaves your phone

While a task runs, the app sends the task you typed, screenshots of the screen it is working on, and a list of the controls on that screen to our server, which passes them to an AI model (currently GLM by Z.ai, or Claude by Anthropic when configured) to choose the next action. If you add your own model key in Settings, these go straight to that provider instead, under their terms.

What we keep

Our server does not store screenshots, tasks or results after the model has answered. We do not sell data, show ads, track you across apps, or ask you to create an account. Your own model keys stay in the iPhone Keychain.

What the agent will not do

It never types passwords, codes or card numbers: it hands the phone back to you. Before anything that sends, pays, orders, approves, cancels or deletes, it asks for your Allow.
